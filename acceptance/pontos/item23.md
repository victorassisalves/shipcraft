## Item 23: dashboard

- Booleans are not numeric times/tokens or integer dates.
- Reviews with nonnumeric tokens count as reviews without a token measurement.
- Visible metric labels identify values independently of CSS/units.
- Daily dates may use YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY.
- Port refusals end in the three-line format; codes are not required in terminal output.
- Automatic updates may use HTML refresh or a local ten-second timer.
- Reading changes no content/mode/mtime and creates no directory; atime is not compared.
